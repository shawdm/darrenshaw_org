## Looking At

1. Julie F Hill's [Through Machines & Darkness](https://juliefhill.co.uk/Through-Machine-Darkness) uses data to create
   art. In this case satellite data.
2. I've come across [Train Jazz](https://www.trainjazz.com) before. I lost just as much time playing with it the second
   time.
2. I'm a fan of Chris Guest's [Oil Paintings](https://www.chrisguest.co.uk/oilpaintings), they're modern pinups that
   are as fun as they are glamorous. I love the consistency in the models he works with. And, although I'm biased, I do have
   a [favourite](https://www.instagram.com/p/DF53evqIlbb).
3. Keith Tyson is another artist I follow and is always inspiring. His [Generative Universe](https://www.hauserwirth.com/hauser-wirth-exhibitions/keith-tyson-the-generative-universe/) obviously looks awesome.

## Reading

1. [Consider Phlebas](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/8935689-consider-phlebas) has been recommended to me
   multiple times. It's 1980s sci-fi space opera revolving around AI. It was... okay.
2. [AI companies will fail. We can salvage something from the wreckage](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/ng-interactive/2026/jan/18/tech-ai-bubble-burst-reverse-centaur).
   I've read the same article so many times that it's taken me six months to get to this instantiation of it and I
   almost didn't bother. But it did raise a point that you can't (at least in the US) copyright AI generated content,
   which means companies (e.g. Disney)
   can't use it because they'd have to give _Avengers: Bring It On Again Too Fast Too Furious 2033_ away for free. What?
   Interesting.
3. [Hybrid Search With Reciprocal Rank Fusion In Apache Solr](https://sease.io/2026/03/hybrid-search-with-reciprocal-rank-fusion-in-apache-solr.html).
   A deep cut for people who work with Solr. I think there's something clever that could be done with this to combine
   different types of searches that all contribute to a page on an e-commerce or marketplace site.
4. [Fifteen Clocks](https://www.chrbutler.com/fifteen-clocks). This is the sort of post I really love. Made me think
   about measuring time in a whole different way and inspired me to start playing.
