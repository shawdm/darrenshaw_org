I haven't seen it for 30 years, but I have a vivid memory of this Alicia Silverstone picture. This was mid-90s. Clueless.
Cher. Fake Plastic Trees. Whatever.

![Alicia Silverstone by Carlo Dalla Chiesa](/images/notes/2026/alicia-1.avif)

It was part of Carlo Dalla Chiesa's [Seventeen](https://www.seventeen.com) magazine shoot, though it doesn't really fit
in with the rest of the set, which look more of their time.
The [Funkeesentials](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1992-09-04-vw-6512-story.html) t-shirt would be popular
today. Its ghost is still around in [Stüssy](https://uk.stussy.com).
