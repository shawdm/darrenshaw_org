## Reading

1. [The Trainspotter's Notebook](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/61398440-the-trainspotter-s-notebook) I'll
   always take pleasure in hearing people who can do something really well or know a lot about a subject, whatever it is. I
   listened to the audiobook of The Trainspotter's Notebook which included audio clips of some of the moments and train
   sounds. Joyful.
2. [Best Foot Forward](https://thebeautyoftransport.com/2026/11/11/best-foot-forward-accessible-footbridges-at-stations-part-1/)
   is an article about accessible footbridges on the UK rail network. Specifically, it's a criticism of their lack of
   beauty. Another train-related article by someone who knows their stuff.
3. [AI Isn't Replacing SREs. It's Deskilling Them](https://newsletter.signoz.io/p/ai-isnt-replacing-sres-its-deskilling).
   Interesting comparison with airline pilots being
   mandated a minimum manual flying time. When you need an airline pilot, you really need one.
4. [EV Listening - How electric cars sound](https://www.nplusonemag.com/online-only/online-only/ev-listening). They once
   gave cars fake horse heads so as not to scare the other horses.
5. [Creepy crawlies](https://people.kernel.org/monsieuricon/creepy-crawlies). The pain of dealing with crawlers and AI
   agents.
6. [Who Are We Recommending To? Recommender Systems in the Agentic Web](https://research.atspotify.com/2026/9/who-are-we-recommending-to-recommender-systems-in-the-agentic-web).
   Spotify Research on the shift from recommendations made for humans to recommendations made indirectly via agents.
7. [Brownfield Agentic Engineering](https://addyosmani.com/blog/brownfield-agentic-engineering/). Yet another insightful
   Addy Osmani article.
8. [Principles for effective slides](https://martinfowler.com/articles/never-send-slides/slide-principles.html). I've
   read versions of this same idea multiple times. I've not seen the idea of having a companion data site instead of handouts or detailed slides.
9. [Where We'll Live in the Future](https://www.chrbutler.com/where-we-will-live-in-the-future). This article is about
   houses, but really it's about looking back at what we thought the future would be.
